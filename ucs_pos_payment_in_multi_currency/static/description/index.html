See It In Action

1. Path to activate currencies and configure exchange rates.

2. Setup and configuration for INR (Indian Rupee) currency.

3. Setup and configuration for EUR (Euro) currency exchange rate.

4. Path to enable Multi-Currency feature in Odoo settings.

5. Shop-wise configuration: Add allowed payment methods and supported currencies for the POS.

6. In POS: Add a product (e.g. $100) to the cart and proceed by clicking the Payment button.

7. On the Payment screen, select a payment line to reveal the Multi-Currency option.

8. Click on Multi-Currency: A popup appears allowing you to select a currency and view its configured exchange rate in real-time.

9. Choose the desired currency and click "Add" to process the payment in the selected currency.

10. Payment is completed in the chosen currency. To view the session report, click the top menu (3 lines) and select "Close Register".

11. The Closing Register popup displays all payments including multi-currency totals. Click the "Daily Sale" button to download the report.

12. The downloaded Session Report PDF clearly outlines the total amount collected per currency.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about the Track POS Payment in Multi Currency module.

1. What is the purpose of the POS Payment in Multi Currency module?

This module allows you to accept point-of-sale payments in various currencies, automatically converting amounts based on your configured exchange rates.

2. Can I configure custom exchange rates?

Yes, you can define and update exchange rates for any supported currency within the Odoo settings.

3. Does this module support multiple foreign currencies?

Yes, you can configure as many currencies as needed. Customers can pay using the currency of their choice, and the module handles the conversion.

4. Will the payment be recorded in the company currency?

Yes, while the customer pays in their preferred currency, the backend accounting entries and reports accurately reflect the amount in your company's base currency.

5. Is any additional POS configuration required?

You simply need to activate the Multi-Currency feature in your settings, define your exchange rates, and select the allowed currencies for the POS in the shop configuration.

6. Can customers see the converted payment amount?

Yes, when selecting a foreign currency during payment, the system displays the converted amount and the applied exchange rate in real-time.
